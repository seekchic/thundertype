About

Every glyph is drawn as a lightning discharge — a fractal channel grown along the letter's skeleton, then branched and tapered. The weather of the chosen day and place sets every parameter.

The seed comes from the date and the coordinates, so the same day in the same place always regenerates exactly the same typeface.

CAPE (convective available potential energy) is only published for the last 92 days. Outside that window the charge is estimated from rainfall, wind and humidity, and the readout says so.
Calendar view draws one A per day, so a whole month of weather can be read at a glance.
Data: Open-Meteo.
